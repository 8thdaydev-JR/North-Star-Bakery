What we're known for

North Star Bakery has been serving the local community since 2000.
We are dedicated to bringing our friends and families fresh bakes goods using high quality, organic, ethically sourced products
We want to give back to the community that has given so much to us, every night inventory is taken and unsold goods of acceptable quality are donated to our local food bank!
